Harvest fractions

Every harvest takes a row of russell_harvest_lookup.csv, which holds each row's exact value and source; harvest_lookup_row on each event names the one used. The generator's draw from a grain-harvest prior is dropped. Wheat, alfalfa and hay take the harvest lookup the other calibration sites use (s3://carb/management/plant_traits/harvest_lookup.csv, dated 2026-08-06, md5 defbff0c0b86ae9ff6db07d520813538), the values the monitoring harvest product carries, rounded to three decimals as Akash gave them on calibration#10. Corn and tomato take what the site's own samples say the harvest removed instead (Aritra, 2026-10-31; below), and the script recomputes both from the package. This departs on purpose from the shared lookup David asked for on calibration#10: the site's samples are source data, which comes first in David's fill order (tools/event_prep/README.md, "Filling missing events"), and the residue left in the field is what feeds soil carbon. Every row takes 100% of the biomass as removed or to litter, so a harvest ends the crop; an intermediate alfalfa cutting does not (below). David suggested DSSAT parameters if more values were needed; none were.

Tillage

The package has no tillage or cultivation table, but it logs dated soil passes in operations_pesticides.csv: Mulch_Beds (153 rows) and Perfecta (6), herbicide incorporated into the beds, as a rule before planting (the 2013 passes come after the tomato transplanting), and Incorporate (189), herbicide incorporated by cultivating between the beds after tomato transplanting. Its metadata describes the 2003 to 2007 standard versus conservation tillage split (all included data are from the standard halves). Tautges et al. 2019 (Methods 2.1 to 2.3) describe the operations and when they happen relative to harvest, planting, cover crop termination and compost, but not on which day, apart from the wheat straw, or how intense they are. So, in David's order, the logged passes keep the package's dates, the wheat straw disking keeps the paper's month, the fallow diskings, which nothing dates, are spaced evenly through the fallow, and the other dates and every intensity come from the monitoring tillage product near Russell Ranch: monitoring_tillage_near_russell.csv, made by tools/event_prep/russell_monitoring_tillage.R from the v5.0 tillage, harvest and planting products and the v4.1 crop table, for the 11,290 parcels within 25 km, 2016 to 2023. Known limitations

  • Harvest fractions differ from the other sites. Corn and tomato take the site's own shares (above), so Russell no longer uses the shared lookup's F16 and T32 rows, departing from the shared lookup David asked for on calibration#10: the site's samples are source data, which comes first in David's fill order, and the residue left in the field is what feeds soil carbon (Aritra, 2026-10-31). Wheat still takes G2, which removes 0.713 although the paper incorporates the straw; the package has no straw weights to replace it with.
