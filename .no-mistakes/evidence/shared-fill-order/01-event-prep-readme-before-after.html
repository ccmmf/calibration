Filling missing events

When a site's records lack an event that is known to have happened (a crop that was harvested, a cover crop that was incorporated), or lack its date or amount, the generators fill it in this order (David, calibration#10):

  1. The paper or its source data. Use what they record, including dates relative to another recorded operation (a termination before the next planting) and medians over the site's own records of the same system.
  2. The monitoring product (s3://carb/management/). For a field and season it covers, use that field's values. Before 2016, when it starts, use average calendar or relative dates and amounts for comparable crops and operations in the same area.
  3. Guidance documents, as a last resort; ideally their information is already in the monitoring product, as it is for fertilization.

This applies to amounts as well as dates. Every filled or imputed event says where it came from (source, provenance_class, provenance_note).
